# My Aquarium

A cozy 3D aquarium you can play right in your browser. Fill a tank with fish, decorate it your way, keep it sparkling clean, and raise baby fish in the nursery.

**[Play My Aquarium](https://unofficialartworks.github.io/AquariumGame/)** — free, no ads, no sign-up.

## What you can do

- **Collect fish and sea creatures.** Start with a few friendly fish and work your way up to seahorses, jellyfish, a sea turtle, an octopus and a few legendary surprises.
- **Decorate.** Place plants, corals, castles, shipwrecks and more anywhere in the tank, then choose the scene behind it, the gravel and the stand it sits on. Some decorations are gadgets with handy perks, like an auto-feeder.
- **Feed your fish.** Sprinkle food and watch them swim over to eat. Hungry fish get first bite. Special treats make fish sparkle, glow, or zoom around.
- **Keep the tank clean.** Scrub algae off the glass, vacuum the gravel and change the water. Better cleaning tools unlock as you go.
- **Raise baby fish.** Two grown fish can become friends in the nursery and welcome a clutch of eggs. Babies take their shape from one parent and their colors from the other, and once in a while a rare color appears.
- **Fill your Fishpedia.** Every species you meet goes in your collection book, with rewards for milestones.
- **Earn coins and level up.** Happy fish release coin bubbles (tap them for a bonus), and you can sell grown fish. Each level unlocks something new in the shop.

Your aquarium keeps swimming while you're away, and your progress saves automatically.

## Tips

- Drag to look around the tank, and pinch or scroll to zoom.
- Tap a fish to meet it, rename it, or follow it around.
- On iPad or iPhone, open the game in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and launch it from the icon to play full screen.

## Your progress and privacy

Your aquarium is saved only in your own browser on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads and no purchases, and your game data never leaves your device. (The page does load its font from Google Fonts.) Clearing your browser's site data will reset your aquarium, and saves don't move between devices or browsers.

## For developers

Built with React, Three.js (via React Three Fiber), TypeScript and Zustand. Every fish, decoration, effect and sound is made in code, with no 3D models or audio files.

```sh
npm install
npm run dev        # start a local copy
npm test           # run the tests
npm run build      # build the static site into dist/
```

Pushes to `main` are checked and published to GitHub Pages automatically.
