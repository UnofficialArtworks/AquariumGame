# Aquarium games: what the best ones do, and how ours can beat them

Research pass, 2 October 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

## The short version

Our game already does several things none of the big ones do together: a real 3D tank in the browser, free with no ads, fish that never die, hands-on cleaning, breeding where you can see which parent gave the body and which gave the colour, and rare morphs. That is a strong base. The gaps are the things that make the top games *sticky*:

1. **Sound and calm.** Every well-loved aquarium game gets praised for its music first. We have sound effects but no music, no "just watch" mode, and night is a manual toggle.
2. **A reason to come back tomorrow.** Daily/weekly goals, achievements and seasonal events show up in nearly every hit. We have none.
3. **Visitors and surprises.** The single most copied idea in cozy collecting (Neko Atsume, AbyssRium) is "set things out, and special creatures come to see them." We don't have it.
4. **Showing off.** The highest-rated recent aquarium game (Tiny Aquarium, 96% positive) is built around visiting other people's tanks. We can't share a tank at all yet.
5. **A long-term goal.** Zen Koi 2's koi-to-dragon ascension and Splash's "grow it, then release it to the wild" give players something past the level cap. Ours stops at level 30.

If we add those five while keeping what players hate about the others out (ads, paywalls, fish dying, chores, hidden requirements, fear of missing out), this can honestly be the best one in its lane: the cozy, kid-friendly, decorate-and-care aquarium.

## What the winners have in common

- **Music and atmosphere come first.** AbyssRium, Zen Koi 2, Tiny Aquarium and Fish Game are all praised for soothing music before anything else. It's the cheapest way to feel premium.
- **The world keeps living while you're away.** Real-time growth, offline earnings, day and night that follow the real clock. (We have offline earnings; night is manual.)
- **Collections with layers.** A species list, then colour variants of each species, then ultra-rares (Chillquarium, Fishlets, Zen Koi). Players chase completion.
- **Small daily reasons to return.** Daily and weekly quests, login gifts, task sets, seasonal events.
- **Surprise visitors.** Rare things show up because of what you placed (Neko Atsume), or hidden creatures appear once you meet a condition (AbyssRium).
- **Somewhere to show it off.** Visiting tanks, a daily showcase, reactions, screenshots.
- **A payoff beyond "number goes up".** Dragons in Zen Koi, releasing fish to the wild in Splash, growing a shop into an oceanarium in Aquarist.
- **Fish feel like characters.** Talking fish in Fishdom, personalities in Fish Game, schooling and territorial behaviour in Behind Glass and Megaquarium.

## What players hate (don't copy)

- **Ads and paywalls.** This is the top complaint for AbyssRium and Splash. Our no-ads, free game is already a selling point, so keep it that way.
- **Fish dying, sickness, expensive medicine.** This is Fish Tycoon 2's biggest complaint. Lost Garden's cozy-design essay lists threat and loss as coziness killers. We never kill fish; keep it.
- **Chores.** "Lots of cleaning" is the knock on Aquarist. Cleaning should stay satisfying and optional, and get *easier* as you progress.
- **Hidden requirements.** AbyssRium players complain they can't tell how to unlock fish. Every secret needs a hint.
- **Fear of missing out.** Limited-time event fish that never return hurt. AbyssRium gets credit for bringing event fish back each year.
- **Crossbreeds that don't make sense.** Breeding results should be explainable at a glance. Ours already is: body from one parent, colour from the other.

## Where we already lead

- **Real 3D on the web.** No install, and it works on iPad from the Home Screen. Most rivals are 2D, mobile-only or paid PC.
- **Free with no ads, and fish never die.** That's rarer than it should be.
- **Hands-on care that feels good.** Glass scrubbing, gravel vacuum, water changes, upgradeable tools. Fishdom has a sponge; nobody else gives you this much to touch.
- **Breeding you can read.** Body from one parent, colour from the other, plus rare morphs (Golden, Pearl, Midnight, Aurora). That fixes Fish Tycoon's "random crossbreed" complaint.
- **Gadgets** that give bonuses, a **Fishpedia** with milestone rewards, **coin bubbles** you can tap for double, a **photo mode**, and fair feeding where hungry fish eat first.
- **Content:** 37 species, 39 decorations, 8 scenes, 8 gravels, 9 stands, 7 tools, 8 foods. That's fewer species than the PC sims (130–400), but they're hand-built 3D creatures with personality, which matters more for kids.

## Recommendations, ranked

Each has a rough size (S = a day or two, M = about a week, L = bigger) and the game it borrows from.

### 1. Music, a relax mode and a real day (S–M) — AbyssRium, Chillquarium, Tiny Aquarium, Fishdom

- **Music:** add a gentle looping soundtrack with its own volume or mute, separate from sound effects. Use two or three tracks: day, night and nursery. Choose royalty-free or commissioned tracks and keep the files small.
- **Relax mode:** one tap hides all the UI. The camera drifts slowly around the tank, and fish do their thing. Tap anywhere to come back. Think screensaver: on iPad, kids will leave it running.
- **Real clock:** let lights follow the time of day (dawn, day, dusk, night), with the current night toggle as an override. Some fish could be more active at night: the jellyfish glow, the octopus comes out.
- **Why first:** it's cheap, and it's the most-praised thing in every top game.

### 2. "Tank wishes" and achievements (M) — Tiny Aquarium quests, Fish Tycoon task sets, Deconstructor of Fun on daily goals

- **Daily wishes:** three a day, picked from what the player can actually do. Examples: "Feed the guppies 3 times", "Scrub the glass sparkling clean", "Pop 5 coin bubbles", "Take a photo of a fish near a castle". Small coin and XP rewards, plus a little bonus for finishing all three.
- **No pressure:** no streaks that break, and missed wishes just roll over. The cozy rule is that nothing bad happens if you skip a day.
- **Achievements:** long-term badges in the Fishpedia or on a trophy shelf. "Raise 10 babies", "Own every legendary", "Clean 100 smudges", "Find all 4 morphs of one species".
- Each wish gives a gentle nudge toward a feature kids might never find on their own: the nursery, gadgets, tools.

### 3. Visitors (M–L) — Neko Atsume, AbyssRium

The standout idea. Special creatures **visit** your tank for a while, attracted by what you placed, and leave a gift when they go. Examples:

- **Treasure Crab:** visits when a treasure chest is placed, and leaves coins.
- **Hermit Crab:** tries on your shells.
- **Pufferfish Postman:** brings a letter (the daily wishes).
- **Ghost Shrimp:** comes at night to the Glow Cave.
- **Sea Dragon:** only visits a tank with kelp and ruins.

How it works:

- Visitors aren't owned and don't count toward the 30-fish cap, so there's no stress.
- A **Visitors page** in the Fishpedia shows silhouettes with a hint for each ("Loves shiny things…"), so nothing is hidden without a clue. That fixes AbyssRium's complaint.
- It turns decorating into a puzzle, gives every decoration a second purpose, and rewards checking in.
- **Code fit:** decorations already carry gadget bonuses (`state/bonuses.ts`). Visitors would be a new sim system like `coins.ts`, with a spawn check against placed decorations and creature rigs reused from `scene/creatures`.

### 4. Style sets and a tank beauty score (S–M) — Fishdom

- **Style tags:** every decoration already has style tags (`styleTags`: adventure, sparkle, nature, classic, sci-fi), but gameplay never uses them. Show them in the shop, and maybe split them into friendlier set names (Pirate, Jungle, Ancient, Zen).
- **Beauty score:** show it in the decorate drawer, with bonus beauty for grouping a style, like Fishdom.
- **Rewards:** beauty adds to coin bubbles, and set milestones unlock special visitors (#3) or a matching background.
- Gives decorating a goal without forcing it, and makes theming feel clever.

### 5. Share your tank (M; L for the online version) — Tiny Aquarium

- **Step 1, no server needed:** pack the tank into a share link or code: layout, fish, scene, gravel, stand. Opening it shows a **read-only visit** of that tank with its fish swimming. Add a "Copy tank code" button and a nicer photo card with the tank name. Works on GitHub Pages as-is.
- **Step 2, later, needs a backend:** a "Tank of the Day" showcase, and sticker reactions that visitors can leave.
- **Kid safety:** if this goes online, keep the social layer to preset stickers and no free text. That keeps it safe for children and avoids the legal requirements that come with collecting kids' data. Lost Garden makes the same point: social contact should be opt-in, with cheap friendly gestures like stickers.

### 6. Fish with personality (M) — Fishdom, Fish Game, Megaquarium

- **Traits:** give each fish one or two (Shy, Playful, Greedy, Curious, Show-off), shown on its card and affecting behaviour. Shy fish hide by plants and castles. Playful fish chase bubbles. Greedy fish beat others to food, which works with the fair-feeding system.
- **Favourites:** a favourite decoration (the fish hangs around it, with a heart pop) and a favourite treat.
- **Petting:** tap a fish to wave. It turns, wiggles and gets a small mood boost, at most once per fish per hour.
- **Schooling:** tetras, danios and rainbowfish are happier in groups of 5+, with a tiny coin bonus and the school swimming together. This is Megaquarium's shoaling rule, made gentle.
- **Fun facts:** add a fact line to each Fishpedia page (Splash's Aquapedia). That's educational without lecturing.

### 7. A long-term payoff: the Open Ocean (L) — Splash, Zen Koi 2

- **Release:** a fully grown fish can be **released** to the Open Ocean instead of sold, for a stamp and a reward.
- **The ocean view:** a separate reef where your released fish live forever, and it fills up over time. Your legacy.
- **Why:** gives purpose to extra babies from the nursery and to the 30-fish cap. Players stop feeling sad about letting fish go, because they're still there.
- **Prestige:** releasing fish could fill an "ocean meter" that unlocks prestige rewards past level 30, like new rare morphs or a third tank.

### 8. Deeper breeding (M) — Zen Koi 2, Fishlets, Chillquarium, Behind Glass

- **Patterns:** add one more inherited trait, like stripes, spots or a fin shape, so pairs can make looks neither parent has. The rule must stay readable, e.g. "pattern comes from either parent; matching patterns can make a rare one".
- **More rare variants:** add variants per species (Painted, Rainbow), and show the full grid per species in the Fishpedia so collectors can see the gaps.
- **Breeding challenges:** "Make a Midnight clownfish". A puzzle, like Fish Tycoon's charts, but always explainable.

### 9. Seasons and events, with no fear of missing out (M, ongoing) — AbyssRium, Tiny Aquarium

- **Holidays:** decor and treats on the local calendar (Spring Bloom, Summer Reef, Spooky Seas, Winter Lights), and the event's visitor or morph.
- **Everything comes back:** event items return every year, so nobody misses out forever.
- No server needed, since it's driven by the device's date.

### 10. Helpers and automation late game (S–M) — Insaniquarium pets, Aquarist's complaint

- **Bigger crew roles:** the snail, shrimp and pleco could earn real jobs. One collects missed coin bubbles (Stinky the Snail), another slows algae, another tidies waste.
- **Late-game upgrades:** a "Filter Pro" or "Auto-Scrubber" so cleaning becomes a choice, not a chore, by level 20+. Kids who love scrubbing still can.

### 11. Small but valuable

- **Photo requests:** "a photo of 3 different fish together", rewarded the way AbyssRium rewards photography.
- **A third tank:** at a high level, add a themed tank such as a Koi Pond or Coral Reef, with its own species set. Fishdom's multiple tanks and Chillquarium's tank upgrades show players love expanding.
- **Fishing minigame:** an optional way to find new fish and rares. Tiny Aquarium's is its most-praised feature. Lower priority, since it's a whole new mode.
- **Fish naming:** offer name suggestions when a baby hatches. Fishdom and our nursery both do naming, so make it a moment.

## Suggested order

**Phase 1: feel and habit** (biggest wins for the effort)
- Music, relax mode and the real-time day cycle (#1)
- Daily tank wishes and achievements (#2)
- Style tags and the beauty score (#4)

**Phase 2: the surprise and show-off layer**
- Visitors with the hint page (#3)
- Share-a-tank links and visits (#5, step 1)
- Fish personalities, petting and schooling (#6)

**Phase 3: depth and long-term goals**
- Open Ocean releases and prestige (#7)
- Pattern genes and the variant grid (#8)
- Seasonal events (#9)
- Helper jobs and auto-cleaning upgrades (#10)
- A third tank, fishing and online showcase (#11, #5 step 2)

Guardrails for all of it: no ads, no real-money randomness, no fish death, no chores you can't skip, no requirements without a hint, no limited-time-forever content.
